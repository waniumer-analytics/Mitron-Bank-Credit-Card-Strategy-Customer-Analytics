## Business Objective

1.  Identify high-value customer segments.
2. Analyse customer spending and payment behaviour.
3. Evaluate income utilisation.
4. Identify target customers for a new credit card.
5. Recommend product features based on customer insights and market research.

## Key Insights

1.  4,000 customers analysed across 6 months.
2.  Overall income utilisation: 42.82%.
3. Salaried IT Employees: Highest utilisation at 51.04%.
4. 25–45 age group: Core customer and spending segment.
5. Top spending categories: Bills, Groceries & Electronics.
6. Mumbai: Largest customer base and strong utilisation.
7. Primary target: Salaried IT professionals aged 25–45.

## Tools & Technologies

1. Tableau – Interactive dashboards & visual analytics.
2. Excel – Data exploration and preparation.
3. Python – EDA and analytical validation.
4. SQL  – Data analysis and KPI generation.
